Description:

Kare-Kare is a prestigious and deeply comforting Filipino stew featuring a thick, golden sauce made from toasted ground rice and rich peanut butter. It is traditionally slow-cooked with tender cuts of beef, oxtail, or tripe, alongside a fresh assortment of vegetables including eggplant, string beans (sitaw), banana blossoms (puso ng saging), and pechay (Chinese cabbage)

Because the peanut sauce itself is intentionally mild and nutty, Kare-Kare is incomplete without its signature pairing: sauteed shrimp paste (bagoong alamang). A spoonful of salty, savory bagoong mixed into the creamy sauce creates a perfect flavor contrast that makes this a centerpiece dish for special occasions and Sunday family gatherings.

Ingredients:

  • 2 lbs oxtail, cut into pieces
  • 1/2 lb tripe, cleaned and cut into pieces
  • 1/2 lb beef shank or stew meat, cut into chunks
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/2 cup peanut butter
  • 1/4 cup toasted ground rice (or 2 tbsp rice flour)
  • 6 cups beef broth or water
  • 1 small eggplant, sliced
  • 1 cup string beans (sitaw), cut into 2-inch pieces
  • 1 banana blossom (puso ng saging), sliced
  • 1 cup pechay (Chinese cabbage)
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Bagoong alamang (fermented shrimp paste) for serving
